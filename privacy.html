Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 22, 2026

At ApnaGhar Rentals ("we", "our", "us"), your privacy matters to us. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you use our website, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using this website, you agree to the practices described below.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information only when you choose to share it with us:

  • Contact details — your name, phone number and email address when you call us, message us on WhatsApp, or send an enquiry form.
  • Requirement details — the type of property you are looking for, your preferred area and your budget.
  • Communication records — copies of messages or emails you send us, so we can follow up on your request.

This is a static website. It does not secretly track or profile its visitors.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to your rental enquiries and answer your questions.
  • To find and suggest properties that match your requirements.
  • To arrange property visits and complete rental documentation.
  • To send you updates about listings you asked about — we do not spam.

3. Cookies

This website does not use advertising or tracking cookies of its own. Our hosting service (GitHub Pages) may collect limited, standard technical information such as browser type and pages visited, for security and performance purposes.

4. Sharing of Information

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information. We share your details only in these cases:

  • With a property owner or our staff — and only the details needed to arrange a visit or deal you requested.
  • When required by law, court order or a government authority.

5. Third-Party Links

Our website contains links that open WhatsApp, your email app or your phone dialer. Once you leave this website and open another app or service, that service's own privacy policy applies.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable precautions to protect your information from misuse, loss or unauthorized access. However, no method of communication over the internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Data Retention

We keep your enquiry details only as long as needed to serve your request or as required by law. You can ask us to delete your information at any time.

8. Your Rights

You have the right to ask what information we hold about you, to correct it, or to have it deleted. To make such a request, email us at mariyamtech6@gmail.com. We will respond as soon as possible.

9. Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a new "Last Updated" date.

11. Contact Us
